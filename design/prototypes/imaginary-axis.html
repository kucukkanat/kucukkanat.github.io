02:00 · A LONG TIME AGO

My father wakes me up in the middle of the night.

“What is the square root of minus one?”

I didn’t know. It took me years to see that i is not a number on the line. It is a turn off it.

This site is plotted on that turn. The real axis is the work. The imaginary axis is everything else.
