Six whales swim through the sky, each carrying its own world: a forest with a waterfall lake, a desert oasis, a snowy pine tundra, a jungle with cascades, an autumn grove with ruins and a cherry-blossom garden.

Every whale is built by script in Blender, true to its species: the blue whale's flat U-shaped head and throat pleats, the sperm whale's box head and narrow jaw, the beluga's melon, the humpback's long knobbly flippers, the bowhead's arched mouth and the minke's pointed snout. Flippers and fluke bend like soft tissue as it swims, and the trees, water and particles on its back lean and slosh as it banks, climbs and dives.

They also wander the sky outside this cabin. Fly the galleon close and one may swim alongside.
