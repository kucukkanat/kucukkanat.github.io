A pod of whales swims through the sky, each carrying a world grown from its seed: forests with waterfall lakes, desert oases, snowy tundra, jungles, autumn groves, cherry-blossom gardens, lava calderas, giant mushroom groves, coral lagoons, mangrove swamps and overgrown ruins. Every day brings a new pod, and any seed you type makes a whale you can share by link.

The bodies are built by script in Blender, true to their species: the blue whale's flat U-shaped head and throat pleats, the sperm whale's box head and narrow jaw, the beluga's melon, the humpback's long knobbly flippers, the bowhead's arched mouth and the minke's pointed snout. Each individual then gets its own size, proportions, mottling, scars, barnacles and moss.

The island is alive to the whale's motion. Every tree and lantern swings on its own spring, lakes stay level as the whale tilts, slosh when it surges and spill over their banks, waterfalls run down its flank, boulders roll off in a dive, and birds, grazers and fish scatter when it lurches. At night the lanterns glow, the birds roost and fireflies come out; in a storm it rains.

They also wander the sky outside this cabin. Fly the galleon close and one may swim alongside.
